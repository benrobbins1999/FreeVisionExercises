How to use

  1. Put on red/blue 3D glasses. By default the red lens goes over the left eye (you can change this in Settings).
  2. Sit about an arm's length from the screen, with the screen at eye level.
  3. Look at the purple box. A smaller square will seem to float toward you near the top, bottom, left or right.
  4. Press the matching arrow key ↑ ↓ ← → (or tap the buttons).
  5. Right answers pull the red and blue boxes apart. Wrong answers bring them closer together. Try to keep seeing one box as they separate.

This is a practice tool, not a diagnosis or a replacement for care from an eye doctor or vision therapist. Follow the schedule your clinician gives you. Short sessions (about 5–10 minutes) are typical. Stop and rest if you get a headache, eye strain, dizziness or double vision that won't go away.
